# Course Canon

This file is the implementation source of truth for the **AI for Managers** course. When older notes, pull requests, roadmap copy, search metadata, or README text conflict with this file and the current student-facing dashboard, update the stale material instead of preserving both versions.

## 15-week progression

| Week | Primary topic | Student dashboard artifact / milestone | Major course assessment |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI foundations and problem framing | Dashboard Starter Version | Discussion 1 |
| 2 | AI tools, integrations, APIs, and privacy | AI Tools | Quiz 1 |
| 3 | AI-assisted research and verification | Research & Know | Week 3 knowledge check |
| 4 | AI productivity and human review | AI Productivity | Discussion 2 |
| 5 | AI-assisted managerial decisions | Manager Decision Assistant | Quiz 2 |
| 6 | AI ethics | AI Ethics Checker / Risk Assessment | Discussion 3 |
| 7 | Responsible AI and governance | Responsible AI Policy | Test 1 · Weeks 1–7 |
| 8 | Customer evidence and decision briefs | Customer Evidence Decision Brief | Discussion 4 |
| 9 | Human-reviewed AI workflows | Human-Reviewed AI Workflow | Quiz 3 · Weeks 8–9 |
| 10 | AI use, plagiarism, attribution, and disclosure | AI Use / Disclosure Log | Discussion 5 |
| 11 | Christian perspective and values-based judgment | Values-Based AI Decision Framework | Discussion 6 |
| 12 | AI and the workforce | Workforce Impact Map | Test 2 · Weeks 10–12 |
| 13 | Implementing AI in an organization | AI Implementation Plan | Implementation assignments |
| 14 | Future of AI, testing, and release readiness | Dashboard Testing and Revision Record | Quiz 4 · Weeks 13–14 + Discussion 7 |
| 15 | Final integration and defense | Final Integrated AI Management Dashboard | Cumulative Final Exam |

The course therefore has four major quizzes, two mid-course tests, and one cumulative final exam. Small lesson checks and practice quizzes do **not** count toward the four major-quiz total.

## Discussion schedule

Only these seven weeks carry a required course discussion:

1. **Week 1 · Discussion 1 — Where Should a Manager Refuse AI Assistance?**
2. **Week 4 · Discussion 2 — Should Employees Disclose AI Use at Work?**
3. **Week 6 · Discussion 3 — When Does Using AI Become Unethical?**
4. **Week 8 · Discussion 4 — When Should a Manager Trust an AI Recommendation?**
5. **Week 10 · Discussion 5 — Is Using AI Plagiarism?**
6. **Week 11 · Discussion 6 — Christian Perspective on AI**
7. **Week 14 · Discussion 7 — Is This Dashboard Ready to Release?**

Weeks without a required discussion may still include reflection, peer feedback, or informal class questions, but the dashboard must not label those as a required discussion assignment.

## Weekly quality standard

Every week should give a student enough information to complete the work without developer knowledge. At minimum, the student-facing week should include:

- a clear overview and learning objectives;
- concept-specific lesson content rather than placeholder prose;
- an applied activity, case, or practice task;
- a clearly named dashboard artifact or milestone;
- step-by-step directions and required evidence;
- an example, model, or decision frame when useful;
- submission requirements and grading criteria;
- the assigned quiz, test, or discussion when that week has one;
- resources, reflection, and progress state; and
- visible human accountability, verification, privacy, or risk controls when relevant.

## Naming and synchronization rules

- **Week 3 is Research & Know.** “Prompt Library” is retired as the Week 3 artifact name.
- Weeks 8–15 use the artifact names in `app/course-progression.ts`.
- The student dashboard, Assignments page, Discussion Board, course search, AI Course Assistant, course-structure workspace, translations, README, and documentation must use the same week titles and artifact names.
- Do not keep legacy assignments merely for backward compatibility when they contradict the approved week.
- Major assessment counts refer to the scheduled course assessments above, not every lesson-level knowledge check.
- Student work saved only in the browser must be described as locally saved, not submitted to an instructor or gradebook.

## Release gate

A week is not complete because its title appears in navigation. Before release, confirm that the student can open the week, understand the task, enter or produce the required evidence, save progress, complete the assigned assessment, and understand how the work is actually submitted.
